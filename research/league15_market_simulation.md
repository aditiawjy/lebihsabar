- One unit per fixture; constant hypothetical DECIMAL odds; no staking progression.
- Markets/lines not necessarily offered historically. No historical odds, commissions, liquidity, live entry state or timestamps of goal events.
- Same dataset used for exploration; no independent prospective profitability validation. Zero-goal counts as Even.
